### Added

- **auth:** add database layer, session auth and dynamic RBAC (dfefee6)
- **employees:** manage roles, permissions and employee accounts (5c34d63)
- **settings:** add audit log viewer and owner settings (392ce53)

- **catalog:** add colour variants (2ffcfcb)

- **invoices:** add printable invoices, PDF, sharing and signed links (8a3446b)
- **approvals:** add generic approval engine, inbox and sale voids (7c559e5)
- **vouchers:** add vouchers with approved revisions and POS use (11693cd)
- **kasbon:** add store credit with approved installments (637172b)
- **online-orders:** add marketplace orders with status flow and returns (b0e0192)
- **reports:** add dashboard figures, sales reports and CSV export (c17afeb)
- **housekeeping:** add monthly archive export and marking (545e030)
- **capacity:** add database capacity monitoring (3950da1)
- **ux:** two-step login, secret reveal, shift warning and clearer labels (6ec27df)
- **ux:** thousand-grouped money inputs and POS-like store credit payments (997a52e)

- **ui:** design-system dropdowns and date pickers (cebd576)

- **ui:** open single forms and short details in dialogs, auto-apply filters (b54d6fb)
